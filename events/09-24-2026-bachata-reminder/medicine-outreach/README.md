## Future outreach convention

Use one appropriate editor or community organizer per audience; request their preferred channel and frequency. Submit a term announcement ahead of time, then reminders only where welcomed. Track overlapping audiences, approval and publication so a pending submission does not trigger duplicates. Do not collect individual student addresses, use clinical/on-call lists, or enroll recipients in the club list. No automated follow-up or recurring sending was created.
