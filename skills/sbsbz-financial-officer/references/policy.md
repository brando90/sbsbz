# SBSBZ finance rules and their sources

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/sbsbz/blob/main/skills/sbsbz-financial-officer/references/policy.md>

**TLDR:** Each rule the FO agent applies, with where it comes from.
- **Official:** a current Stanford, ASSU (Associated Students of Stanford University), GSC (Graduate Student Council) or OSE (Office of Student Engagement) page. Quotes were checked against the raw page text on 10-09-2026 unless marked otherwise. Rows P13-P25, G10-G14 and R4-R5 were added 10-14-2026 from the Finance 101 course text, the full GrantEd Help Center and the GSC 2026-27 Funding Policies.
- **Club notes:** meeting notes or guidance the FO relayed. Confirm those before they decide money.

Re-check every academic year.

**Source key**
- KB = GrantEd Help Center article `https://sse.frontkb.com/en/articles/<id>`
- Finance 101 = the STARS course SSE-2024 (lesson names as in the course; see `finance-101-notes.md`)
- `/209` = <https://www.assu.stanford.edu/209/Funding-GrantEd-Training>
- `/206` = <https://www.assu.stanford.edu/206/Graduate-VSO-Grants>
- `/196` = <https://www.assu.stanford.edu/196/ASSU-Funding>
- NSF = OSE non-student framework, <https://ose.stanford.edu/student-orgs/policies/non-student-involvement/non-student-framework>

The old `assu.stanford.edu/finances/...` URLs return 404.

## Paying people
| # | Rule | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | An outside instructor or DJ is paid by a GrantEd **Service Payment** after the service. Documentation: "Email/text agreeing on payment amount, invoice with itemized charges, or event flyer." | Official | KB 8112065 |
| P2 | The payee needs a W-9. GrantEd emails the payee a W-9 request when the FO enters their name and email; resends go through `banking@sse.stanford.edu`. | Official | KB 8112065, 8112129 |
| P3 | Peer-to-peer apps "such as Venmo, Zelle, or Apple Cash are not allowed for any services from people." An organizer who pays an instructor by Venmo should not expect reimbursement. | Official | KB 8112129 |
| P4 | Outside payees get a check, by mail or pickup. Direct deposit requires Axess, so it is for Stanford students only. | Official | KB 2852097, 8755457; ASSU FAQ /211 |
| P5 | "If you have not been approved prior to your payment request, your request will be rejected." | Official | KB 8755457 |
| P6 | Service payments of $1,000 or more need OSE approval; $10,000 or more needs OSE review plus a contract. | Official (research report; the table was not re-quoted) | KB 8112065; OSE contracts page |
| P7 | "DO NOT TRY TO SELF REIMBURSE." | Official | KB 8112065 |
| P8 | Instructors paid by service payment are independent contractors of the ASSU. An instructor employed by Stanford needs pre-approval from their department. | Official | NSF |
| P9 | An **ongoing** coach or instructor needs a Registration Form (the Non-Student Involvement Form in CardinalEngage), a HireRight background check and Core 10 Policies training. "Significant" contact means more than twice a month. | Official | NSF |
| P10 | GSC honoraria cannot pay on-campus scholars: faculty, visiting scholars, postdocs or students. Officers may not be paid for normal club activities. | Official (research report; not re-quoted 10-09-2026) | GSC 2026–27 funding guidelines; KB 3316289 |
| P11 | Pay instructors per class, after the class is taught, attaching the written agreement on the amount. | Club practice | 05-03-2026 FO meeting |
| P12 | One GSC funding request may cover many sessions, one funding line per session (a past officer filed 10 weeks in one request). There is no $200 cap: requests above $300 per session have been approved. Each session still needs its own invoice, and several instructors may be paid if each sends separate invoices. GSC is very slow in 2026–27, so submit early. (Replaces the earlier "each invoice under $200, submitted separately" note, 09-25-2026, which no official page supports.) | Club practice (past officer) | Past officer Hikari, email to Brando, 10-20-2026 |
| P13 | Anyone paid for a service, **Stanford students included**, is paid by Service Payment so tax information is collected. "DO NOT pay the individual yourself and attempt to submit a reimbursement request after." | Official | Finance 101 (SSE-2024), lesson "Payments" (read 10-14-2026) |
| P14 | GrantEd's Tax Info column must read "Yes" (the vendor submitted their W-9) before Capital Group reviews the request. | Official | Finance 101 video "Understanding Payment Requests on GrantEd" |
| P15 | Rush processing costs $35, charged to the group's 2800 account. | Official | Finance 101 video "Submitting Payment Requests Tutorial" |
| P16 | One receipt per payment line. Its proof of payment goes on the same line. All files for a line attach to that line, so don't create extra lines for documents. | Official | KB 10398657; Finance 101 "Payments" |
| P17 | Receipts are itemized and whole: one complete PDF, or one clear photo, per receipt. Never split, crop, black out or alter one. Only PDF, PNG and JPEG are reviewed. | Official | KB 10398657, 2851969; Finance 101 "Payments" |
| P18 | Online orders also need proof of shipment or delivery, or a posted (not pending) charge. | Official | KB 10483265; Finance 101 "Payments" |
| P19 | A partial amount says "Partial Reimbursement" in the line or summary description, never only in internal comments. Tips are capped at 20%, and anything over is deducted as a partial reimbursement. | Official | KB 10398657, 2851777, 8755457 |
| P20 | Lost receipts: 3 per group per academic year, $75 each, one per person, paid only from the 2800 account. Proof of payment is still needed. | Official | KB 2852225 |
| P21 | A student is reimbursed only after opting in or out of GrantEd direct deposit. Everyone else (non-students, invoice and service payments) is paid by check. | Official | KB 2573121; Finance 101 "Getting Started" |
| P22 | The President (or VP) must approve requests of $1,000 or more and any reimbursement the FO submits for themselves. | Official | Finance 101 "Getting Started"; KB 8755457 |
| P23 | Advanced payments are paid by check. An itemized list and receipts follow within two weeks, and leftover money is deposited back. | Official | KB 8112001 |
| P24 | Gift cards: at most $50 each, store cards only, never as thanks to students. Gifts or prizes worth more than $100 need each recipient's W-9. | Official | KB 2852289, 2716545, 8112129 |
| P25 | Spending over about $100 per person may need a partial reimbursement or tax information. Club money is not the officers' money: no extravagant meals, merch or expedited shipping. | Official | Finance 101 "Dashboard" and "Ethical Behavior" |

## GSC funding
| # | Rule | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | "The GSC does not provide retroactive funding." | Official | /206 |
| G2 | "Funding requests must be submitted by Sunday, 5 pm" to be voted on at that week's GSC meeting. | Official | /206 |
| G3 | Lead time: ≤$500 needs 3 meetings before the event and no attendance; ≤$1,000 needs 4 meetings; >$1,000 needs 5 meetings and attendance at a GSC meeting. Above $500, each line needs a quote or invoice. Hence monthly requests under $1,000. | Official | /206; GSC Funding Policies (2026-27 link on /196) |
| G4 | ASSU applications "must be submitted at least one month before the event date"; requests a week or less ahead may be denied. | Official | /196 (also relayed by FO 09-21-2026) |
| G5 | Every GSC-funded event goes on the GSC Funding Calendar at least 14 days ahead (mandatory). | Official | GrantEd Quick Grant question text; GSC guidelines |
| G6 | "At least 30% graduate versus undergraduate participation." | Official | /206 |
| G7 | GSC wants an attendee list; a photo is not enough. | Club notes (reviewer feedback relayed by FO, 09-23-2026) | FO chat |
| G8 | Unspent allocations disappear at fiscal-year end. Official pages say only that the fiscal year turns over June 30, with no extensions past it. | Observed in the statement (06-30-2025 "GSC takebacks") | VSO statement export |
| G9 | Food: up to $20 per student per meal, snacks up to $8; a quote is required above $500; tips at most 20%. | Official | KB 3316353, 2851777 (re-read 10-14-2026) |
| G10 | Quick Grant hard cap: $7,000 a year for groups with 70% or more graduate members; $7,000 × the graduate share for 30-69%. ASSU's page says "up to $7,500 per organization per academic year" across bodies. | Official | GSC Funding Policies 2026-27; /196 |
| G11 | A group that got no GSC money in the previous fiscal year is advised to stay under $2,000 a year, $500 per event and 50 claimed attendees, unless it asks for an exception at a GSC meeting. | Official | GSC Funding Policies 2026-27 |
| G12 | A group holding more than a reasonable emergency amount in its general (2800) account is not recommended for Quick Grants until it spends that down. | Official | GSC Funding Policies 2026-27 |
| G13 | Quick Grants are reviewed only in weeks 1-9 of each quarter. Funds arrive 1-2 weeks after approval, and must be in the account before anything is bought or booked. | Official | /196 |
| G14 | ASSU and Stanford Fund (TSF) accounts don't roll over: unused money is reclaimed at the end of the academic year. General (2800) and university-grant accounts do roll over. Spend only on the approved line items, and change them with a Funding Modification. | Official | Finance 101 "Dashboard"; KB 3190081 |

## Roles and training
| # | Rule | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | "Finance 101 For VSO Leaders" (STARS SSE-2024, about 25 minutes) is required annually for the FO, President, Co-Presidents and VP; the hold lifts the next business day. | Official | KB 2637057; ASSU /209 ("required to complete Granted Training annually", re-read 10-14-2026) |
| R2 | "To prevent abuse & fraud, you cannot hold both the President & FO role for the same organization." | Official | Finance 101 "Getting Started" |
| R4 | Only the FO creates and submits requests. Presidents and VPs share GrantEd's "President" role: they view everything and approve at the President stage. A Stanford Affiliate can only file their own reimbursement. Roles come from the CardinalEngage roster (exactly President, VP or FO, marked Active) through a nightly sync. | Official | KB 2539905, 1318849; Finance 101 "Getting Started" |
| R5 | Misused funds go to the Office of Community Standards, and can be escalated to SUDPS (Stanford's police department). Individuals are held accountable even for group violations. | Official | Finance 101 "Violations & Consequences" |
| R3 | Non-students "should never be considered 'members' or take leadership roles." | Official | NSF |

## Not found on official pages (10-09-2026)
- A reimbursement deadline after purchase.
- A "Compensation" account. The club's own statement does show a `Compensation - VSO` line used for weekly classes in 01-2026, so match whichever account the approved funding line uses.
- A date for the end-of-year takeback. Finance 101 (10-14-2026) confirms ASSU and TSF money is reclaimed at the end of the academic year (G14), but gives no date.
- Sales tax on reimbursements.
