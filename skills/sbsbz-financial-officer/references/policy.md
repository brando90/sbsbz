# SBSBZ finance rules and their sources

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/sbsbz/blob/main/skills/sbsbz-financial-officer/references/policy.md>

**TLDR:** Each rule the FO agent applies, with where it comes from. **Official** means a current Stanford, ASSU (Associated Students of Stanford University) or GSC (Graduate Student Council) page. **Club notes** means meeting notes or the FO's relayed guidance; confirm those before they decide money. Re-check official pages each academic year.

| # | Rule | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | President, Vice President and FO must complete "Finance 101 For VSO Leaders" (STARS code SSE-2024, ~25 min) before handling money in GrantEd; holds lift the next business day. | Official (via guide) | GrantEd Help Center article 2637057, quoted in Lorena's onboarding guide |
| 2 | GSC does not fund retroactively; request before the expense. | Official (via guide) | GSC graduate VSO grants page, quoted in the guide |
| 3 | GSC reviews requests submitted by Sunday 5 PM at that week's meeting. | Official (via guide) | Same |
| 4 | Requests over $1,000 need committee review and someone (via the FO) at a GSC meeting; so keep monthly requests under $1,000. | Official threshold; the splitting strategy is club practice | Same; 05-03-2026 FO meeting |
| 5 | GSC-funded events must be on the GSC Funding Calendar at least 14 days before the event. | Official (GrantEd question text) | Quick Grant question 2, quoted in the guide |
| 6 | Events must be open to all graduate students, with about 30% or more graduate participation. | Official (via guide) | GSC page, quoted in the guide |
| 7 | Submit at least one month before the event; requests one week or less ahead may be denied automatically. | Club notes (FO relayed as "official ASSU guidelines", 09-21-2026) | FO chat |
| 8 | GSC wants an attendee list; a photo is not enough. | Club notes (GSC reviewer feedback relayed by FO, 09-23-2026) | FO chat |
| 9 | Unspent GSC allocations are taken back at fiscal-year end (June 30). | Observed in the official statement (06-30-2025 "GSC takebacks") | VSO account statement export |
| 10 | Pay instructors per class after the class is taught. Attach the emailed quote and the instructor's acceptance to the Service Payment. | Club practice | 05-03-2026 FO meeting |
| 11 | Postdocs (e.g. a postdoc instructor) cannot be paid from the group's funds. | Club notes | 05-03-2026 FO meeting |
| 12 | Outside community members who pay the club do so through `assuepay.stanford.edu` (group ID), not personal Venmo/cash; two dance groups were penalized for letting non-students pay for lessons outside university channels. | Club notes | 03-2026 meeting; Tango Club advice in the Transition Planner |
| 13 | Each instructor invoice must be under $200 and submitted separately. | **Unverified belief** (Brando, 09-25-2026) | Confirm with SSE before relying on it |
| 14 | The President cannot also be the FO. | Club notes | Lorena's onboarding guide |
