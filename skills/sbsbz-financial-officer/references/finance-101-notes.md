# Finance 101 For VSO Leaders: what the course says

**Doc link:** <https://github.com/brando90/sbsbz/blob/main/skills/sbsbz-financial-officer/references/finance-101-notes.md>

**TLDR:** These are notes on the yearly GrantEd officer training (STARS SSE-2024, version `sse-2024-v01`, read 10-14-2026), in course order, so an officer can review it quickly and agents can apply it. It is about 25 minutes, self-paced, in 10 lessons with 3 videos, an ethics scenario quiz and a final True/False acknowledgment. The President, VP and FO must each finish it **every academic year**, and GrantEd lifts its hold the next business day. Brando completed it for 2026-27 on 10-20-2026; see "Retaking it" at the end. Each officer takes the course themselves; these notes do not replace it.

## 1. ASSU (Associated Students of Stanford University)
- Every student is a member. The ASSU funds 700+ VSOs (Voluntary Student Organizations).
- Its branches are the Undergraduate Senate (UGS) and the Graduate Student Council (GSC), each with 15 elected members, plus the Executive, the Constitutional Council, and Stanford Student Enterprises (SSE).
- Funding decisions belong to the funding administrators: the GSC Treasurer and the UGS Appropriations Committee. SSE's Capital Group (CapGroup) gives only preliminary guidance.

## 2. SSE and Capital Group
- CapGroup runs GrantEd, reviews every transaction for compliance, trains officers and keeps records for IRS audits.
- Its office is Old Union Suite 103, open 9-5 on weekdays. Email `banking@sse.stanford.edu`.
- ASSU ePay collects dues and payments electronically. Charitable donations cannot be deposited through GrantEd and must be cleared with OSE (Office of Student Engagement) well ahead.

## 3. GrantEd: getting started
GrantEd has three roles:
- **Stanford Affiliate** (every student): files their own reimbursements and deposits.
- **Financial Officer:** the only role that creates and submits requests for CapGroup review. The FO tracks returned requests and plans the budget with the President.
- **President** (Presidents and VPs share this role): views everything and approves requests of $1,000 or more and any reimbursement the FO files for themselves.

The same person can never be both President and FO.

Every student who wants a reimbursement must first opt in or out of direct deposit in GrantEd; until then, no student reimbursement can be created for them. Non-student reimbursements and invoice or service payments are paid by check only.

## 4. Dashboard (video)
- The info icon shows the officers on record, and the money icon shows the group's funding by year.
- **My Queue** holds the FO's unsubmitted and returned requests, and the President's approvals. **Recent Activity** lists the last 30 requests.
- **Accounts** lists each fund:
  - **General (2800):** the group's own income (dues, sponsors, awards). It can be spent at the officers' discretion within policy, and it rolls over.
  - **University-collected:** department grants. Spend it on the grant's purpose; it rolls over.
  - **TSF (undergraduate groups only):** approved line items only. It does not roll over.
  - **ASSU accounts** (Quick, Annual and reserve money): approved line items only, split by category such as 2900 Honoraria. They do not roll over; unused money is reclaimed at the end of the academic year.
- Wait until funds are approved **and posted** before spending, because nothing is retroactive.
- Expenses above about $100 per person may need a partial reimbursement or tax information.
- Use an account only for the exact approved line. A photographer is Honoraria, not Supplies. Anything else needs a Funding Modification first.

## 5. Payments (2 videos)
- **Paying a person for a service, a Stanford student included, is a Service Payment** (so tax information is collected). Never pay them yourself and claim it back.
- **Payments tab:** "Assigned to me", "In progress" and "All". Assign a request to yourself (the person-pencil icon) before editing it. A request CapGroup returned is orange, and a rush request is yellow.
- The **Assignment group** column shows where a request sits in the queue.
- The **Tax Info** column must say **Yes** (the vendor filed their W-9) before CapGroup reviews the request; "No" means follow up with the vendor. N/A means no W-9 is needed.
- **New Payment Request** (FO only):
  1. Pick the type; Student Reimbursement is the most common.
  2. Enter the payee's SUNet ID and a summary description that names the event.
  3. Answer Rush yes or no. Rush costs **$35 from the 2800 account**.
  4. Click Next. A request number appears.
- **Payment lines:**
  - Add one line per receipt or document, dated as on the receipt. The document type is Receipt; use Lost Receipt only under the lost-receipt policy.
  - The vendor is as printed on the receipt, and the amount is exactly the receipt total. If you claim less, write "Partial Reimbursement" in the line description.
  - To pay one line from two accounts, use the account distribution split, and make the split amounts add up to the line amount.
- **Documents:**
  - an itemized receipt or invoice, plus proof of payment: a posted charge, the card's last four digits, or a $0 balance due
  - for online orders, also proof that the item shipped or a posted charge
  - for mileage, a map of the route with its total miles
  - PDF, PNG or JPEG only. Attach every file to the same line; never add lines just for documents, and never split one receipt into several images.

## 6. Ethical leadership
- Follow the policies, be transparent and fair, share decisions, and address problems directly.
- Tip only as much as needed. Choose the cheapest shipping and flights, negotiate vendor rates, and deposit refunds back into the club account.
- Don't spend on fancy meals, lots of officer merch, personal add-ons or expedited shipping, and don't keep club items.
- Never return reimbursed items for cash or build a slush fund.

## 7. Presidents' and FOs' duties
- **FO:** submit requests accurately and promptly, with full documentation.
- **President:** double-check requests and watch transactions and balances often.
- **Both:** budget conservatively, avoid end-of-year spending sprees, and keep decisions transparent. Co-Presidents check GrantEd so that no one officer can misuse funds.

## 8. Rationalizations
"I didn't have time", "I didn't have a choice", "I didn't know", "It wasn't my responsibility" and "Everyone else does it" are the excuses the course names. Its fixes are to state your intentions, seek other views, practice scenarios and make a personal promise.

## 9. Scenario lessons
What the scenarios teach:
- When a restaurant adds a mandatory 18% gratuity, tip 18%. Up to 20% is allowed; never 25%.
- Never alter a receipt or signed copy, even by 2%: that is fraud.
- If an undergraduate group's bill includes alcohol, file a partial reimbursement that leaves out the drink and its tax and tip.
- For a club meal, pick a noodle shop over Nobu or a Michelin restaurant. $600 for 10 people ($60 each) is too much.
- If the FO wants soda for a personal party, it goes on a separate transaction they pay for. No trading favors.
- If an unexpected $5 item shows up on a receipt, ask the FO about it before approving.

## 10. Violations, acknowledgment and completion
- Violations can lead to a talk with CapGroup or OSE, a report to the Office of Community Standards, or escalation to SUDPS (Stanford's police department).
- Individuals answer under the Student Conduct Charter, and groups under the Stanford Group Accountability Process (SGAP). The outcome can be anything from probation to expulsion, and a graduating senior's diploma can be held.
- **Final acknowledgment:** True or False, "this training is a basic introduction and does not cover all financial policies". The answer is True: the full rules are in the FO Knowledge Base (<https://sse.frontkb.com/en>), from CapGroup and at Managers' Office Hours, and funding questions go to UGS or GSC.
- **Completion:** exit with BOOKMARK/EXIT in the top-right corner. Axess emails a certificate within minutes. If the hold needs lifting the same day, send that certificate to `banking@sse.stanford.edu`.
