# SBSBZ Website Design Brainstorm

## Context
Stanford Bachata Sensual & Brazilian Zouk (SBSBZ) — a Stanford student-run dance community focused on connection, technique, and creative expression through Bachata Sensual and Brazilian Zouk. Inspired by the Stanford Ballroom website's clean, informational structure but elevated for a Latin/partner dance aesthetic.

## Idea 1: "Warm Nocturne" — Intimate Evening Social Aesthetic

**Design Movement:** Inspired by editorial nightlife photography and warm ambient lighting — think the golden glow of a social dance floor at midnight.

**Core Principles:**
1. Warmth over coolness — amber, terracotta, and deep burgundy tones evoke the intimacy of partner dance
2. Cinematic framing — large hero imagery with film-grain overlays and vignette effects
3. Readable elegance — high-contrast text on dark backgrounds, never sacrificing legibility
4. Organic flow — sections flow into each other with diagonal cuts and wave dividers rather than hard lines

**Color Philosophy:** A palette rooted in the warmth of a dimly lit dance studio. Deep charcoal (#1A1A1A) as the primary background, with warm amber (#D4A574) as the accent, burgundy (#8B2252) for CTAs, and cream (#F5E6D3) for body text. The warmth signals connection and passion — core to Bachata Sensual.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed hero sections with asymmetric text placement. Content sections alternate between left-aligned narrative blocks and right-floating cards. The schedule uses a horizontal timeline rather than a traditional table.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Subtle film-grain texture overlay on hero sections
2. Diagonal section transitions using CSS clip-path
3. Warm gradient borders on cards (amber to burgundy)

**Interaction Philosophy:** Slow, intentional hover effects that mirror the deliberate movements of Bachata Sensual. Cards lift gently on hover with a warm shadow bloom. Navigation items underline with a smooth left-to-right wipe.

**Animation:** Sections fade in from below with a gentle parallax offset. Text elements stagger in word-by-word on hero sections. Scroll-triggered reveals use ease-out-cubic timing at 600ms — unhurried, like the dance itself.

**Typography System:** DM Serif Display for headings (elegant, warm serifs), paired with Source Sans 3 for body text (clean, highly readable). Heading weights: 400 for display, body weights: 300/400/600.

## Idea 2: "Fluid Motion" — Dance-Inspired Kinetic Design

**Design Movement:** Inspired by Swiss kinetic typography and contemporary dance poster design — movement captured in stillness.

**Core Principles:**
1. Motion as identity — every element suggests movement through rotation, curves, and dynamic positioning
2. Bold contrast — stark black-and-white foundation with a single vivid accent color
3. Typographic drama — oversized display type that breaks conventional alignment
4. Negative space as choreography — strategic emptiness creates rhythm on the page

**Color Philosophy:** Near-black (#0D0D0D) and pure white (#FFFFFF) create the stage. A single vivid coral-red (#E8453C) serves as the spotlight — used sparingly for CTAs and key highlights. This mirrors the dramatic lighting of a dance performance where a single spotlight illuminates the dancers.

**Layout Paradigm:** Broken grid with overlapping elements. Hero text wraps around circular image masks. Content sections use a magazine-style layout with varying column widths. The team section uses a masonry grid with staggered heights.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Circular image masks that suggest the circular movements of Zouk
2. Oversized numbers/letters as decorative background elements (50% opacity)
3. Thin animated lines that trace paths across sections, suggesting dance floor patterns

**Interaction Philosophy:** Snappy, energetic micro-interactions. Buttons scale up with a slight rotation on hover. Images reveal with a circular wipe. Navigation uses a full-screen overlay with staggered text animation.

**Animation:** Elements enter with spring physics (slight overshoot). Scroll-triggered animations use intersection observer with 200ms stagger between siblings. Hero text uses a split-character reveal with rotation.

**Typography System:** Clash Display for headings (geometric, bold, modern), paired with Inter Tight for body (compact, clean). Headings at 700 weight, body at 400/500.

## Idea 3: "Stanford Cardinal Warmth" — Collegiate Community Aesthetic

**Design Movement:** Inspired by modern university club websites with a warm, approachable twist — think Stanford's brand warmth meets a welcoming community hub.

**Core Principles:**
1. Approachable authority — professional enough for a Stanford VSO, warm enough for a dance community
2. Content-first clarity — information hierarchy that helps newcomers immediately understand what SBSBZ offers
3. Community-centered — photos of real people and events take center stage
4. Stanford-adjacent branding — nods to Stanford's visual identity without copying it

**Color Philosophy:** Warm off-white (#FAF7F2) as the canvas, with Stanford Cardinal (#8C1515) as the primary accent, deep espresso (#2D1810) for text, and a soft terracotta (#C4704B) as the secondary accent. The palette says "Stanford" while the terracotta warmth says "Latin dance."

**Layout Paradigm:** Clean, wide content sections with generous padding. A sticky top navigation with a clear CTA. Content organized in a clear top-down narrative: Hero → About → Classes → Schedule → Team → Join. Uses a traditional but refined grid with occasional full-bleed image breaks.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Rounded image frames with subtle warm shadows
2. A persistent "Join Us" floating CTA that pulses gently
3. Section headers with a small decorative underline in terracotta

**Interaction Philosophy:** Friendly and responsive. Buttons have a gentle scale + color shift on hover. Cards elevate with a warm shadow. Smooth scroll between sections. Toast notifications for "coming soon" features.

**Animation:** Fade-up entrance animations with 400ms duration. Staggered card reveals in grid sections. Hero text types in with a cursor effect. Subtle parallax on background images (restrained, 10% offset).

**Typography System:** Playfair Display for headings (classic, editorial serifs that feel warm), paired with Nunito Sans for body (rounded, friendly, highly legible). Headings: 700/800, body: 400/600.
